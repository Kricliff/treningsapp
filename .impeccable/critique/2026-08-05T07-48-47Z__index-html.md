## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: The app does not read as a generic fitness-app template — exercise names, cue text, the exact SATS 4-day split, the specific calorie/macro numbers, and the "send only a Strava summary, never the full log" privacy choice are clearly authored for one real program and one real person. But the *visual* language (dark card-on-card, single green accent, system sans stack, generic bar/heatmap widgets) is interchangeable with any dark-mode utility app — specificity lives entirely in copy and data model, not in any typographic, color, or compositional choice.

**Deterministic scan**: CLI scan of `index.html` found 1 finding — a `layout-transition` warning (`transition: width` on the week-goal progress bar, line 183; causes layout thrash, should animate `transform`/use `grid-template-rows` instead). Browser-injected detection across the Fremgang, Logg, and Kosthold tabs found substantially more: **undersized UI text below the 11px legibility floor on every tab sampled** (chart values, stat-labels, and all 6 bottom-nav labels sit at 9.6–10.9px), a **WCAG-failing contrast pair** (`#2fd47a` on `#1e6b45`, 3.3:1 against a 4.5:1 requirement) on the rest-timer button and the bodyweight reminder banner, and a mixed `roboto`/`arial` font split flagged as possibly just a fallback-stack artifact rather than a real defect.

**Visual overlays**: Injection succeeded and produced the findings above, but the temporary local server and detector overlay were stopped after evidence collection (per this command's own invariant), so no live overlay remains open in a browser tab right now — the findings below are the evidence, not a standing visual artifact.

A few browser findings were flagged by the evidence-gathering pass itself as likely artifacts, not real defects: some `text-occlusion` hits matched the detector's own overlay-label text rather than app content (self-detection), and several Kosthold-tab findings duplicated Fremgang-tab elements — likely prior-view DOM left hidden rather than removed when switching tabs, not distinct Kosthold-view issues.

## Overall Impression

A functional, honestly-built personal tool where the product truth (real program, real numbers, additive-safe data handling) is genuinely well expressed in copy and behavior — but the visual craft hasn't caught up: text sits below the legibility floor everywhere, one real color pair fails contrast, and the two newest/least-reviewed surfaces (the calorie calculator and the Historikk delete flow) both violate the app's own stated principle of never silently losing or misrepresenting data. The biggest opportunity is closing that gap between "the copy explains the safety principle" and "the UI actually enforces it."

## What's Working

- **The PR banner** ("🏆 Ny personlig rekord på Brystpress (maskin): 40 kg! Sterkt jobbet.") — specific, warm, appears exactly where the user looks to confirm effort paid off.
- **"Dette er et startpunkt, ikke en fasit..."** framing on the calorie plan card — turns a cold macro output into permission to adjust, matching the product's own philosophy instead of presenting numbers as prescriptive.
- **The additive-data posture is stated in copy, not just built into architecture** — "Økter slås sammen med det du allerede har lagret" directly reassures against the exact failure mode (silent overwrite) that has already cost this user real data once.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Unconfirmed destructive delete in Historikk**
Why it matters: "Slett denne økten" renders as a plain red text link right under "Send til Strava" with no confirm step found — one mis-tap permanently removes a logged session, directly contradicting the product's own "never a silent overwrite" principle.
Fix: Require a confirm step (native `confirm()` or two-tap pattern), and separate it visually/spatially from the low-stakes "Send til Strava" action.
Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`

**[P0] Calorie target has no validation and the progress bar can lie**
Why it matters: The kcal field accepts "10" with no bounds-checking, and "574 / 10 kcal" then renders as a full green "on target" bar — visually identical to a legitimately met goal, on the newest and least-reviewed surface in the app.
Fix: Add sane min/max bounds on the target inputs, and a visually distinct warning state (not green) when actual intake implausibly exceeds target.
Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] Widespread undersized UI text below the 11px legibility floor**
Why it matters: Detector-confirmed on every tab sampled — chart values, all stat-labels, and all 6 bottom-nav labels sit at 9.6–10.9px, well under a readable floor, on a tool used one-handed mid-workout.
Fix: Raise the minimum font size for stat-labels, chart labels, and bottom-nav text to at least 11–12px.
Suggested command: `/impeccable typeset`

**[P1] Contrast failure on the rest-timer button and reminder banner**
Why it matters: `#2fd47a` text on `#1e6b45` measures 3.3:1, below the 4.5:1 WCAG AA requirement — this is the app's own accent color failing its own background, not an edge case.
Fix: Lighten the background or darken/brighten the text on `.rest-btn` and `.reminder-banner` until contrast clears 4.5:1.
Suggested command: `/impeccable audit`

**[P1] No tab identity in the persistent header**
Why it matters: The header ("Treningslogg / Muskelbygging & fettforbrenning") is identical across all 6 tabs. One-handed, mid-workout, after an interruption, the only orientation cue is a small bottom-nav color change — the dominant top-of-screen space gives zero context.
Fix: Swap the subtitle for the active tab's name/context, or add a persistent tab label near the top.
Suggested command: `/impeccable clarify`

**[P1] Treningskonsistens grid is unreadable in its current near-empty state**
Why it matters: A GitHub-style contribution grid with no day/date-axis labels, showing ~95 empty cells against one green dot — for a light-history account it reads as a large dark rectangle conveying almost nothing.
Fix: Add week/date axis labels and a lighter empty-cell treatment so the grid reads as a calendar even at low data volume.
Suggested command: `/impeccable clarify`

## Persona Red Flags

**Casey (distracted, one-handed mid-workout)**
- The "+ sett" / "− sett" / "Hvile 90s" row sits in a long scroll with no reachability consideration for one-handed thumb use — by the time these matter (between reps), they may already be scrolled above the thumb-natural zone.
- The identical header (see P1 above) is specifically a Casey failure: she relies on top-of-screen context to reorient after an interruption, and gets none.

**Riley (stress-tester)**
- Typing "10" into the kcal target was accepted silently and propagated the nonsense value into three surfaces (Kosthold plan card, Kalorikalkulator, Fremgang chart labels) without ever flagging it.
- "Slett denne økten" (see P0 above) is exactly the low-friction destructive action a stress-tester triggers by accident while probing the UI.

**Alex (power user)**
- No bulk or auto-fill entry path for logging sets fast — each set field is a separate tap-in box with no "same as last set" auto-fill despite showing prior-value ghost text, so a power user re-types the same numbers across a rotating 4-day program.

## Minor Observations

- Destructive actions are styled two different ways: "Slett denne økten" (Historikk) is a bare text link, "Slett all data" (Innstillinger) is a proper red-outlined button — same action class, two treatments.
- The native "Velg fil" import button renders in the browser's default light-gray style, the one un-themed control in an otherwise consistent dark UI.
- Dag-tab pills are horizontally scrollable and visibly clipped at the screen edge with only a thin gray bar as a scroll cue.
- "Vis progresjon"/"Vis tips" links share the same blue as "Send til Strava" — link color doesn't distinguish "expand info" from "trigger an external action."
- Kondisjonsdager weekday chips clip the last chip mid-letter with no "more" affordance.
- Bottom-nav icons mix photographic-style emoji (🏋️📈📅📋🥗⚙️) rather than a cohesive icon set, adding visual noise that also renders inconsistently across OSes.
- The `roboto`/`arial` font split the detector flagged is plausibly just a fallback-stack artifact, not a real defect — worth a quick look, not a priority fix.

## Questions to Consider

1. If the calorie target field can silently hold "10" and still render a green "on track" bar, what else touching real health numbers isn't being validated anywhere in the app?
2. The product's own principle demands additive-only, never-silently-destructive data handling — so why does "Slett denne økten" get less protection than "Slett all data," when both are equally permanent in the moment?
3. Given the header never changes across six tabs, what's the current theory for how a distracted, one-handed user re-orients mid-set — the app's own stated primary use case?
